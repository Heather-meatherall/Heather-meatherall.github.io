Hello World! I'm a recent computer science grad who loves learning and gaining new experiences. My main interests are data analysis, web development and QA, but I'll try anything once and I'm not afraid of a challenge!

My journey in tech started all the way back in elementary school during a coding workshop at my local high school. I quickly fell in love with how open-ended it was, and found that in tech often the only limitation is your imagination. I took every course and workshop I could get my hands on, and this path lead me to study computer science at Ontario Tech University. For the past 5 years I've learn about data analysis, front end development, and how to utilize AI to enhance human abilities, rather then replace. I love working on projects that offer interesting and innovative solutions to everyday problems, both in and out of the classroom.

In my free time, I can be found crocheting, playing music, going on public transit adventures or throwing my dnd charatcers into new and exciting situations.
